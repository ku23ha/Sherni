About The Writer

Writing from the quiet spaces between words.

Audrita Mukherjee is a writer and poet whose work traces the tender intersections of love, silence, grief, and self-restoration. Deeply influenced by Bengali literary lyricism and contemporary English verse, she builds sanctuaries in sentences.

She believes that a poem is not an explanation, but an invitation — a porch light left on in the rain.
