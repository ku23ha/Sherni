The River Knows

there is a kind of listening that does not ask the river to explain its drowning. it simply sits on the bank and lets the water speak in ripples. what we hold tightly slips between our fingers. what we leave open learns to stay.

About The Writer

Writing from the quiet spaces between words.

Audrita Mukherjee is a writer and poet navigating the twin currents of English and Hindi verse, grounded in Bengali lyricism. Her poetry explores vulnerability, restorative silence, the geography of memory, and Calcutta rain.

She believes that writing is not a performance for an algorithm, but a quiet porch light left on in the rain. Here, in this private digital garden, the first 50 poems are written solely for herself.

Digital Garden Philosophy

The First 50 Are For Herself

In a digital world that rushes every thought to publication, this garden is a quiet sanctuary. Ideas are planted as seeds, left to root slowly without optimization or expectation.

I wonder why some people feel like places you have visited before you were born.

ख़ामोशी वो नहीं जहाँ आवाज़ न हो, ख़ामोशी वो है जहाँ किसी आवाज़ की ज़रूरत न रहे।

To let a word sit unsaid for three days is to learn what it was really trying to say.
